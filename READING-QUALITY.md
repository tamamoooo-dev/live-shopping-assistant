# Reading quality — 2026-10-22

The engine now separates the contents of one package from the number of packages included in a selling price. Cards show the package count and the price of a package within that offer. Two six-packs of 1.5 L water compare on 18 L. Ten slices in a 200 g cheese package do not turn it into 2 kg.

Search, Browse, the product sheet and comparison consume the same resolved contents. An ambiguous, repeated or stale count withholds unit arithmetic and displays an image-review label. A model-read price only associates quantity evidence with the existing retailer price; it cannot replace that price.

Arabic display recovery can use the retailer caption only when a unique English title prefix anchors it and it contains more confirmed product/brand words. The original model observation and display provenance remain stored. Model and request limits are unchanged.

Validation covered 1,003 Panda crops and 128 samples across 16 other retailers. The assistant visually reviewed 157 images. Of 91 checked quantity references, 79 were correct and 12 were withheld, with no incorrect arithmetic in that sample. A final unused 32-image set produced 17 correct quantities and two withheld results across its 19 measurable cases. Arabic keyword probes improved from 165/251 to 197/251 overall, and from 30/48 to 37/48 in the final set. These are targeted checks, not a claim of perfect full-title recognition.

Engine tests: 99 files. Frontend tests: 18 files. The mobile browser check verifies Arabic deal labels and the 18 L water quantity. Detailed engine evidence: [reading-quality report](https://github.com/tamamoooo-dev/shopping-connector/blob/main/brochure-engine/benchmarks/reading-quality-2026-10-22/REPORT.md).
